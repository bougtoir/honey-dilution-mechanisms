# Dilution-Dependent Antibacterial Activity of Honey

A systematic review and cross-study quantitative evidence synthesis of
published in vitro data on how honey's antibacterial activity varies with
dilution. Current submission target: **Journal of Food Science**
(Original Article).

## Reproduction

Requires Python 3.10+ with `pip install -r requirements.txt` (or the
conda spec in `environment.yml`).

Canonical pipeline: **`make all`** regenerates every submission artifact
from the versioned datasets in `data/` (this is the entry point that also
works inside the distributed package). **`make acquire`** runs the
source-acquisition stages (search/screen/fetch/extract), which require
live PubMed/PMC/Crossref access and rewrite raw snapshots under
`data/raw/` — they are not part of `make all`:

| stage | scripts | purpose |
|---|---|---|
| search | `00_search.py`, `00b_citation_mining.py`, `00c_repo_search.py` | PubMed/Europe PMC/Crossref + citation/data-repository searches; raw JSON in `data/raw/` |
| screen | `01_screen.py`, `02_rank_candidates.py`, `04_eligibility.py` | dedup, title/abstract screen, eligibility classification |
| fetch | `03b_resolve_pmcid.py`, `03_fetch_pmc.py`, `05_parse_tables.py` | PMCID resolution, PMC OA JATS-XML retrieval, table parsing |
| extract | `06_autoextract.py`, `07_manual_extraction.py`, `08_build_dataset.py`, `09_dictionary_rob.py` | rule-based + curated extraction, harmonized dataset, data dictionary + risk of bias |
| analyze | `10_analyze.py`, `10b_analyze_fri.py`, `18_extraction_bias.py`, `24_v4_gates.py`, `25_v5_gates.py` | all quantitative results into `results/models/analysis_results.json` |
| figures | `11_figures.py`, `12b_references_fri.py` | figures (PNG/TIFF/SVG) + Crossref-verified references |
| documents | `27_jfs_cleanup.py`, `28_manuscript_jfs.py`, `29_supplement_jfs.py`, `30_jfs_extras.py`, `31_tables_standalone_jfs.py` | JFS eligibility cleanup, manuscript/supplement/cover letter/standalone tables DOCX |
| package | `32_package_jfs.py` | `submission/jfs_final/` + `JFS_submission_package_FINAL.zip` |

The search/fetch stages require network access and re-query live APIs;
their outputs are persisted under `data/raw/`, `data/interim/` and
`logs/` and are the versioned inputs to all downstream stages.
`data/interim/manual_extraction.csv` is regenerated by `make acquire`
(scripts 07 + 21 + 23, in that order); `make all`/`make rebuild` reuse the
versioned file.

## Outputs (JFS)

- `manuscript/manuscript_jfs.docx` — main text (double-spaced, continuous
  line numbering, author-year citations, CSE "and others" style);
  distributed in the package as `manuscript/JFS_main_manuscript.docx`
- `manuscript/supplement_jfs.docx` — Supplementary Tables S1-S11
  (three-line tables), incl. S11 = bibliography of the 100 included studies;
  distributed as `manuscript/JFS_supplement.docx`
- `manuscript/cover_letter_jfs.docx`, `manuscript/tables_jfs.docx`
  (distributed as `manuscript/JFS_cover_letter.docx` /
  `manuscript/JFS_tables.docx`)
- `submission/jfs_final/JFS_submission_package_FINAL.zip`
- `results/models/analysis_results.json` — single source of truth for
  every number quoted in the manuscript; nothing is hand-entered

## Shared data outputs

- `data/processed/extraction.csv` — 5,111 observation rows from 100
  studies, with table/row/column provenance
- `data/processed/chemistry.csv`, `data/processed/risk_of_bias.csv`,
  `data/processed/data_dictionary.md`
- `data/interim/eligible.csv` — 404 eligible studies
  (381 include + 23 review-flag)
- `logs/` — search logs, PRISMA counts, PMCID resolution

## Estimand note (natural vs artificial honey)

Two summaries exist on purpose and are NOT interchangeable:

- `analysis_results.json → A2` (script 10): raw `natural:artificial`
  ratios over all 25 identified pairs (13 MIC + 10 zone + 2 MBC). Median
  raw MIC/MBC ratio = 0.65; in potency orientation (artificial:natural)
  this is a median of ~1.55 — descriptive only, mixes concentration bases.
- `analysis_results.json → A2_v5` (script 25): the manuscript's primary
  estimand — endpoint-specific, basis-restricted, study-clustered:
  MIC 12 pairs / 5 studies, median potency ratio 1.81 (p = 0.062);
  zone 10 pairs / 2 studies, +0.63 mm; MBC 2 pairs / 1 study.
- Exploratory pair-level Wilcoxon on defined log potency ratios
  (n = 20 of 25; 5 zero-control pairs have undefined log ratios and are
  reported as native-scale differences): p < 0.001, labelled exploratory.

## Legacy entry points (noncanonical, provenance only)

`fri-docs` / `legacy-docs` Make targets and the `*_fri.py`, `*_ijfm.py`,
`1x_*.py` scripts reproduce earlier Food Research International /
International Journal of Food Microbiology / Antibiotics packages. They
are retained for provenance and are NOT part of the JFS pipeline.

## Limitations

No extracted study measured antibacterial activity in an actual food
matrix; extraction covers open-access full texts only (100 of 404
eligible studies). All derived numbers are regenerated by the pipeline;
QC audits live in `qc/` (internal, excluded from the submission zip).
